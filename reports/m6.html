Most of the margin comes from three supplier events (D05, D12, D04). Most avoided spend is D16, where the team spent on freight while cover was enough. Where the co-pilot says "No action" its credit is zero, even if real losses happened (D01, D08, D10): those are losses no mitigation reachable in time would have saved. The structural moves below are the answer to those.

What this does not count. Analyst time saved, avoided expedite premiums beyond the logged ones, customer goodwill, and the structural upside below. What could make it smaller: the 2-year history has a few large events; real feeds are messier than synthetic news; and P(works) comes from the playbook's priors, not from Veltrix's own outcomes yet. The pilot (see the COO one-pager) is designed to measure exactly these.

3 · False-alarm analysis

The brief asks how the co-pilot avoids crying wolf. Answer: four guards, each measured by switching it off and counting the false alarms that come back. The whole team would see about 0.18 alerts a week (≈ 9 a year) and 0.21 inbox alerts a month, out of 35 signals a week read. Test period as built: 0 false-alarm signals, precision 100%, recall 95.3%.

ChangeWhat it fixed
Dampener cues (forecasts that miss, denials, threats, routine weather, good news)Keeps the recurring look-alikes ('typhoon to pass east of Taiwan', 'rumours … denied', 'port workers threaten strike', 'heavy rain forecast for parts of…') out of the inbox.
Network-link gate: a signal must name a supplier, port, lane, chokepoint, region or commodity we depend onDrops world news with no bearing on Veltrix (Odisha cyclones, phishing campaigns, EV sales figures).
Gallium/germanium removed from the semiconductor aliasesRaw materials Veltrix doesn't buy: China gallium export notices no longer link to our chips.
Trade notices: only products in our BOM, only restrictive measuresDrops solar cells, palm polymers, battery passports, aluminium foil and customs-fee notices.
Weather: only official severity ≥ 4Sev 1–3 alerts (routine fog, wind, rain) never alert.

Residual risk. Precision of 100% on synthetic test data will not survive contact with real feeds; expect new phrasings the lexicon has never seen.
