# Labels for the real-news replay
Written blind (shuffled sheet, headline and date only, no co-pilot output) by Claude, which also wrote the triage rules:
spot-check before relying on them.

relevant = 1: a supply disruption, happening or credibly coming, that touches a real port, lane, chokepoint, supplier
country or bought commodity in Veltrix's network (Red Sea/Suez attacks and diversions; typhoons, floods, power cuts at
our nodes; the Taiwan quake hitting chip output; fire or congestion at our ports).
relevant = 0: anything else, incl. Baltimore (not our network), US/EU chip export rules, gallium/germanium/graphite
(raw materials Veltrix doesn't buy), naval and military items not about merchant shipping, business news, opinion,
planned maintenance power cuts, reassurance ("TSMC resumes, no disruption").
