# Veltrix Co-Pilot — Pitch script (2.5 min) + Demo flow (3 min)

The pitch ends on D04: one headline → one supplier → one part → two products → one customer.
The demo shows the same chain again, live, through the four people who should have seen it: each one steps back in turn.

## Part 2 — Demo (3:00 strictly)

**Before you start:** have veltrix-risk-copilot.vercel.app open on Home, zoomed so the back row can read it. One person drives the mouse (Moksh); the speaker for each seat narrates.
**Running thread:** every seat answers its own question on the same screen — *“What does this person need to see, and what do they do next?”*

| Clock | Seat · Speaker | Click path | Say |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0:00–0:15** | Home · Amisha | Home → point at the four seats | “Same company, four people. In D04 each of them saw one brushstroke. Watch what each one sees now.” |
| **0:15–0:55** | **Category manager** · Moksh | Seat *Category manager* → **My desk**: read the one-line summary → open the top alert → open **Where it hits** → open **What to do** → **Propose** | “I own the parts. Top of my desk: how many alerts need action and the rupees at risk. Open one: the headline, the supplier, my part, the products, the customer. The same chain you saw on the slide, built in under a second. Here’s what to do, ranked by net value from Veltrix’s own playbook, with the approver named. I propose the top option.” |
| **0:55–1:25** | **Head of Supply Chain** · Moksh | Seat *Head of Supply Chain* → **My desk** → **Approval queue** → open **One-page briefing** → **Approve** (or **Escalate to COO**) | “I only see what needs my decision. Here’s that proposal, with the rupees and a one-page briefing. I approve it today, not nine days after impact. If it’s above my limit, it goes to the COO in one click.” |
| **1:25–1:55** | **Sales / key-account** · Amisha | Seat *Sales* → pick the customer → **My desk**: read the one-line summary (products at risk, week they fall below 95%) → **Draft note to the customer** → **Copy** | “I own the customer. The co-pilot tells me which products are at risk and from which week they fall below 95% on-time. In D04, Ojas Motors heard it from us after the fact. Now I have a note ready weeks before.” |
| **1:55–2:35** | **COO** · Aryan | Seat *COO* → **Replay** → choose **D04** → open **What it would have recommended** → **Business case** summary | “I check the numbers. This is D04 replayed using only what was known on the day: the alert on 28 January, 19 days early, a ₹32.9 lakh plan, about ₹13.5 crore protected. Across all 18 it’s ₹59.1 crore, ₹11 crore a year net, 22.5 times return including running costs.” |
| **2:35–2:52** | **Ask bar** · Aryan | Type in the Ask bar: *“Which customers miss their 95% on-time target if Penang semiconductor output stops for six weeks?”* | “And anyone can ask a what-if in plain words. The AI reads the question, the engine does the maths.” |
| **2:52–3:00** | Close · Amisha | Stay on the answer | “Four people, one picture, 19 days early. That’s the step back. Thank you.” |

### Timing guardrails
- If you're behind at **1:55**, skip opening the recommendations fold in COO Replay and say only the D04 and all-18 numbers.
- If you're behind at **2:35**, skip the Ask bar and go straight to the close.
- If the HoSC approval queue is empty, open **Alerts** and show the top alert's approver line instead. Rehearse once on the live site, because the queue reflects past approve/reject decisions.
