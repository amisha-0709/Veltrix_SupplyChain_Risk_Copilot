# Persona feedback kit

The brief asks for feedback from at least three personas. This kit is for running those sessions with real people.
**Record only what participants actually say and do. Don't fill in the results yourself.**

- **Who:** one person per persona. Ideally that's someone who does or has done the role. If not, a colleague briefed with the role card below.
- **Time:** 30 minutes each. That's 5 minutes of set-up, 20 minutes of tasks and 5 minutes of wrap-up.
- **What you need:** the live app, the [COO one-pager](../reports/coo_onepager.html) (COO only) and a copy of the scoring sheet per person.
- **How:** share your screen or hand over the laptop, and ask them to think aloud. Don't help unless they're stuck for more than 2 minutes, and note it if you do.
- **Ground rules to read out:** "All data is synthetic. Veltrix is fictional. The tool recommends; people decide. We're testing the tool, not you."

## Persona 1: Category manager (electronics)

**Role card:** You own the semiconductors and PCBs categories. You get the alert first, and you can approve expedites under ₹50 lakh.

| # | Task | What to watch for |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open the **Alerts** inbox. Which alert would you look at first, and why? | Do they use priority, revenue at risk or the response time? |
| 2 | Open that alert's briefing. In one sentence, what's the problem, how much is at risk and what's recommended? | Time to answer. Do they understand the result without reading the working? |
| 3 | Is the recommended option one you could approve yourself? Who else needs to sign off? | Do they spot the approver line? |
| 4 | **Ask:** "Which products stop first if Kaohsiung closes for ten days?" Do you trust the answer? What would you check? | Do they ask for sources or the working? |
| 5 | Record a decision (approve, defer or reject) on the alert. | Is the decision log obvious? |

## Persona 2: Head of Supply Chain

**Role card:** You own OTIF and the mitigation budget, up to ₹2 crore with the CFO. You review the risk register weekly.

| # | Task | What to watch for |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open the **Risk register**. Where is the biggest exposure right now? | Do they read the heat map correctly? |
| 2 | Open **Exposure**. Name two single-source parts you'd dual-source first. | Do they agree with the structural ranking in the M6 report? |
| 3 | **Try a signal:** paste "Fire at a Penang MOSFET plant; output halted for six weeks". Is the impact plausible, and is the plan what you'd do? | What do they challenge: P(works), costs or lead times? |
| 4 | Open **Replay**, D05. Would this have changed what your team did in the actual fire? | Do they trust the counterfactual? |
| 5 | Would you put this in front of your weekly review? What's missing? | Note any gaps they name. |

## Persona 3: COO

**Role card:** You approve anything over ₹2 crore and own the decision to fund a pilot. You have 10 minutes.

| # | Task | What to watch for |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Read the COO one-pager (2 minutes). What's being asked of you? | Is the ask clear without explanation? |
| 2 | Which number on the page do you trust least, and why? | Note whether it's ROI, benefit concentration or synthetic data. |
| 3 | Open one inbox briefing. Is this what you'd want forwarded to you before a call? | Do they see what they need at a glance? |
| 4 | Would you approve the pilot as written? If not, what would change your mind? | Note the conditions they set. |

## After the sessions

1. Put the scores in a table with one row per participant. Keep quotes verbatim.
2. For each issue raised, record whether you fixed it, will fix it in the pilot or won't fix it, and why. Link any fix to its commit.
3. Add a "Persona feedback" section to the M6 report, with real responses only.
